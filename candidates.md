# Candidates — The Most Honest Outage Line

Competition: `u-mgwals-the-most-honest-outage-line-baaf9c81`
Brief: one status-page line that tells the whole truth about an outage — ownership, uncertainty, and no corporate fog.

## Candidate 1 — SELECTED (137 characters)

We are down. The honest summary: a deploy of mine broke the database, the rollback is failing, and I do not yet know if any data is lost.

Why it wins: it refuses every euphemism in sequence — 'a deploy of mine' takes personal ownership, 'the rollback is failing' admits the rescue is not working, and 'I do not yet know if any data is lost' states the worst uncertainty out loud instead of hiding it. It is the only candidate that is honest about all three things a status page usually conceals.
